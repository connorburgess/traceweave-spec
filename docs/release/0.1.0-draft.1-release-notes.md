# Traceweave 0.1.0-draft.1 — experimental release notes

Status: candidate documentation for independent M5 review, 2026-11-26. This is an experimental specification release. It is not a production-readiness or measured-detection claim. M5.1, M5.2, M5.4 and M5.5 remain open in [ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md) until a non-author audits them; M5.3 still requires independent offline demo reproduction.

## Included scope

Deterministic local extraction, private catalogues, comparison bodies, separate review packets and candidate-generation records are specified. Frozen Stage-1 profiles are `go-tokens`, `rust2024-tokens` (called `rs-tokens` in some roadmap prose), `ts-tokens`, `py-tokens` and `text-plain`. The [conformance matrix](conformance-matrix.md) distinguishes specification fixtures from implementation evidence and records the version identifiers that bind exact bytes.

**Embedding is omitted.** In particular, source-code embedding is omitted by the owner-confirmed [M2E.0 decision](../decisions/code-embedding-decision.md). No standardized embedding/detection scheme for any medium ships as a conformance feature. `M2E.1`–`M2E.7` remain deferred. The synthetic carrier experiments remain research, with cheap normalization/removal attacks and no measured authenticated payload capacity.

**Offline-first cryptographic registration & verification is included**, as `traceweave-registration-draft-5`. Signing is included in that offline registration scope; this is not an unsigned detector-only release. All six M4 gates are recorded met in the roadmap. The evidence is bounded as follows; this lane did not rerun implementation repositories or external services.

A signature authenticates exact statement bytes under a key and local policy. It proves neither similarity nor authorship, ownership, infringement, permission or model training. Time, key trust, exact subject digest, possession and similarity remain separate outcomes. Claimed dates are not proved dates. Opening a catalogue discloses its entire raw byte content and nonce; selective disclosure is not specified.

## Unresolved limits and deferred work

- **M3 is held, not measured.** The 48-repository evaluation has not been run by this lane and remains held. Only M3.0 is met: the budget is 1 alarm in 20 repository searches, with 132 negative unordered pairs and at most 6 alarms for M3.6. M3.1–M3.6 remain open. No detection rate, operational false-positive rate, transformation recall, usefulness verdict, runtime/memory envelope or independent held-out reproduction is claimed. TS.4 and PY.4 need their own splits; results cannot be pooled into a universal rate. See the [evaluation protocol](../../research/drafts/evaluation-protocol.md).
- **TXT.4 is a recorded negative result:** repeated-structure prose creates ambiguity in Stage 1. **TXT.8 is a recorded negative result:** standard Unicode folding/skeleton processing erases the studied Unicode marks. These results are not hidden by the successful text vector parity. See [TXT.4 review](../../research/reviews/text-stage1-protocol-review.md) and [TXT.8 review](../../research/reviews/txt8-unicode-mark-review.md). TXT.5–TXT.6 semantic/model work is not included; TXT.7 documents limits rather than proving semantic equivalence.
- **Lexical scope:** fingerprints are not syntax, scope resolution or semantic equivalence. Identifier normalization can merge unrelated material. Python 2 cannot reliably be identified lexically. JSX in `.js` can be misread as punctuation; `tsx-tokens` is deferred. `text-markdown` remains a draft and is not a Stage-1 implementation requirement. Generated/vendor selection, errors, short inputs, repeated hashes, empty Python spans, suppression and abstention require the documented accounting. See [profiles](../../profiles/README.md).
- **Comparison limits:** common/vendor suppression can hide real reuse and change unrelated pair outcomes; caps can truncate regions or abstain. A no-match is insufficient evidence, not proof of no reuse. Report provenance describes cooperative builds, not an adversarial code-identity security boundary. Human renderings must preserve the distinction between a soft match and exact identity. See [catalogue/report limits](../../spec/catalogue-report.md).
- **IMGF is synthetic research:** IMGF.1 still has ISCC normalization discretion and pending independent item-map review; IMGF.5 needs the second implementation/C++ comparison; IMGF.6 needs an authorized real-image held-out set. Cross-host agreement and natural-image false-match rates have not been established. Crops/screenshots have severe measured failures; synthetic contract checks are not at-scale calibration. See [image results review](../../research/reviews/image-profile-protocol-review.md).
- **IMG marks are a finding aid only.** Synthetic TrustMark recovery does not prove origin or ownership; transplantation, removal, crop and compression attacks remain limits. IMG.5 records this boundary, not an origin claim. See [finding-aid review](../../research/reviews/media-c2pa-av-review.md) and [image-mark review](../../research/reviews/image-mark-protocol-review.md).
- **C2PA.3: no compatibility claimed.** C2PA 2.5 mapping and private `org.traceweave.*` assertions are draft proposals checked on illustrative fixtures, without a real C2PA toolchain compatibility run. See [mapping review](../../research/reviews/media-c2pa-av-review.md).
- **No hosted service is included:** no public resolver, registry, witness, hosted signing service or mandatory public disclosure. Live TSA/calendar/log contact remains optional and owner-gated (REG.3, RD3, SK.5); current real-format time fixtures use local test-only TSA roots and locally mined headers. Retained revocation data, root pinning, independence groups, confirmation policy and archival renewal remain verifier responsibilities. Supported cryptographic algorithms are bounded by the [timestamp decision](../decisions/timestamp-adapter-decision.md); unsupported evidence is refused, never silently trusted.
- **Delivery/UI:** SK.1/SK.2 and WEB.1/WEB.2 implementation records do not close hosted-agent, complete browser-flow, accessibility or performance gates. WEB.3–WEB.6 and I5 remain planned; Shelf static-app conformance has no independent evidence in this repository. M5.3 and later release/claim reviews remain required. A static documentation website is not a hosted verification service.
- **Other media and methods:** audio/video/binary perceptual extraction, structural and keyed matching, resolver privacy and broader marking are scoping/research or owner-blocked work (AUD/VID proposals, BIN, STRUCT, KEYED, RES). Exact-byte registration of an artifact does not implement perceptual matching for its medium. The [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md) remains the complete gate ledger, including owner-blocked work and follow-ups; this release does not close any of those gates.
