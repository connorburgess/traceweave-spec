## Included scope

Deterministic local extraction, private catalogues, comparison bodies, separate review packets and candidate-generation records are specified. Frozen Stage-1 profiles are `go-tokens/draft-1`, `rust2024-tokens/draft-1` (called `rs-tokens` in some roadmap prose), **`ts-tokens/draft-2`**, `py-tokens/draft-1` and `text-plain/draft-1`. TypeScript draft-1 is superseded and is not the frozen release wire version. The [conformance matrix](conformance-matrix.md) distinguishes specification fixtures from implementation evidence and records the version identifiers that bind exact bytes.

**Embedding is omitted.** In particular, source-code embedding is omitted by the owner-confirmed [M2E.0 decision](../decisions/code-embedding-decision.md). No standardized embedding/detection scheme for any medium ships as a conformance feature. `M2E.1`–`M2E.7` remain deferred. The synthetic carrier experiments remain research, with cheap normalization/removal attacks and no measured authenticated payload capacity.

**Offline-first cryptographic registration & verification is included**, as `traceweave-registration-draft-5`. Signing is included in that offline registration scope; this is not an unsigned detector-only release. All six M4 gates are recorded met in the roadmap. A signature authenticates exact statement bytes under a key and local policy. It proves neither similarity nor authorship, ownership, infringement, permission or model training. Time, key trust, exact subject digest, possession and similarity remain separate outcomes. Claimed dates are not proved dates. Opening a catalogue discloses its entire raw byte content and nonce; selective disclosure is not specified.

## Unresolved limits and deferred work

- **M3 is held, not measured.** The 48-repository evaluation has not been run by this lane and remains held. Only M3.0 is met: the budget is 1 alarm in 20 repository searches, with 132 negative unordered pairs and at most 6 alarms for M3.6. M3.1–M3.6 remain open. No detection rate, operational false-positive rate, transformation recall, usefulness verdict, runtime/memory envelope or independent held-out reproduction is claimed. TS.4 and PY.4 need their own splits; results cannot be pooled into a universal rate. See the [evaluation protocol](../../research/drafts/evaluation-protocol.md).
- **TXT.4 is a recorded negative result:** repeated-structure prose creates ambiguity in Stage 1. **TXT.8 is a recorded negative result:** standard Unicode folding/skeleton processing erases the studied Unicode marks. These results are not hidden by the successful text vector parity. See [TXT.4 review](../../research/reviews/text-stage1-protocol-review.md) and [TXT.8 review](../../research/reviews/txt8-unicode-mark-review.md). TXT.5–TXT.6 semantic/model work is not included; TXT.7 documents limits rather than proving semantic equivalence.
- **Text failure inventory (TXT.7):** [profile §13](../../profiles/text.md#13-limits-what-the-profile-cannot-detect-or-claim) is the exact authority for the following unresolved limits. These apply even though deterministic vector reproduction passes:
  - Paraphrase is unmeasured by `e-txt-1`; edits inside every 15-token window can remove the winnowing guarantee. Translation/cross-language reuse is expected to miss; the run used 11 English repository files, with no multilingual recall measurement.
  - At shipped `k=8, w=8`, unchanged runs shorter than `k+w-1 = 15` tokens have no shared-fingerprint guarantee. The exact 15-token excerpts matched 11/11 files; `plain-below-kw1` (12 tokens) and `plain-short` (5 tokens, zero k-grams) do not extend that guarantee. Thai/Lao/Khmer/Myanmar non-space scripts and Japanese kana segmentation remain unmeasured for recall; phrase-sized tokens change under re-spacing (`plain-thai`, `plain-japanese`).
  - Repeated structure caused 35/143 transformed positives to abstain as ambiguous despite retained unchanged runs of at least 15 tokens. Distractor insertion every 14 tokens removes only the locality guarantee (still 11/11 matches); insertion every 7 tokens removed shared fingerprints and matches in all 11 files. These are [Stage-1 result rows](../../research/experiments/text-stage1/results/e-txt-1/RESULTS.md), not population detection rates.
  - ZWSP joins words (`alpha` + U+200B + `beta` becomes `alphabeta`, `plain-zwsp-as-separator`). Non-ignorable format characters such as U+FFFB split words across all channels (`plain-format-char-split`). Line-end hyphenation splits `exam-` + LF + `ple`, while soft hyphens disappear (`plain-hyphenation-wrap`); the experiment did not measure line-end dehyphenation recall.
  - NFKC does not unify every typographic variant: apostrophe classes, non-ASCII digits and vulgar-fraction tokenization can remain distinct (`plain-nfkc-compat`, `plain-numbers`, `plain-punct-a`). UTS #39 skeletons deliberately collide across different strings; a skeleton match is not exact-text equality (`plain-confusable-latin`, `plain-confusable-cyrillic`).
  - Draft `text-markdown/draft-2` is a mask set, not a CommonMark parser: it does not decode entities, keeps list numbers/reference labels, tokenizes indented code/HTML/script/style text, and masks inside code spans. These deviations remain outside Stage-1 support; the revised draft still awaits re-review.
  - Common phrases, licences and boilerplate can match unless explicitly suppressed. At floor 1, the 110 ordered inter-file pairs produced 2/110 exact and 4/110 folded/skeleton matches; at floor 2 they were 2/110, 0/110, 0/110. Suppression is advisory and selection-dependent, not a calibrated privacy or accuracy guarantee.
  - Fingerprints are **unkeyed and give no confidentiality**: anyone obtaining them can test guessed sentences. Private storage does not prevent that attack after disclosure. Matches prove no ownership, authorship, permission or direction of copying; no-match proves no absence of reuse. No calibrated full-query false-alert/detection rate or embedded text-mark scheme is included. See the [claim-by-claim text audit](../../research/lanes/09-report-audit.md) and [Unicode-mark negative review](../../research/reviews/txt8-unicode-mark-review.md).
- **Lexical scope:** fingerprints are not syntax, scope resolution or semantic equivalence. Identifier normalization can merge unrelated material. Python 2 cannot reliably be identified lexically. JSX in `.js` can be misread as punctuation; `tsx-tokens` is deferred. `text-markdown` remains a draft and is not a Stage-1 implementation requirement. Generated/vendor selection, errors, short inputs, repeated hashes, empty Python spans, suppression and abstention require the documented accounting. See [profiles](../../profiles/README.md).
- **Comparison limits:** common/vendor suppression can hide real reuse and change unrelated pair outcomes; caps can truncate regions or abstain. A no-match is insufficient evidence, not proof of no reuse. Report provenance describes cooperative builds, not an adversarial code-identity security boundary. Human renderings must preserve the distinction between a soft match and exact identity. See [catalogue/report limits](../../spec/catalogue-report.md).
- **IMGF is synthetic research:** IMGF.1 still has ISCC normalization discretion and pending independent item-map review; IMGF.5 needs the second implementation/C++ comparison; IMGF.6 needs an authorized real-image held-out set. Cross-host agreement and natural-image false-match rates have not been established. Crops/screenshots have severe measured failures; synthetic contract checks are not at-scale calibration. See [image results review](../../research/reviews/image-profile-protocol-review.md).
- **IMG marks are a finding aid only.** Synthetic TrustMark recovery does not prove origin or ownership; transplantation, removal, crop and compression attacks remain limits. IMG.5 records this boundary, not an origin claim. See [finding-aid review](../../research/reviews/media-c2pa-av-review.md) and [image-mark review](../../research/reviews/image-mark-protocol-review.md).
- **C2PA.3: no compatibility claimed.** C2PA 2.5 mapping and private `org.traceweave.*` assertions are draft proposals checked on illustrative fixtures, without a real C2PA toolchain compatibility run. See [mapping review](../../research/reviews/media-c2pa-av-review.md).
- **No hosted service is included:** no public resolver, registry, witness, hosted signing service or mandatory public disclosure. Live TSA/calendar/log contact remains optional and owner-gated (REG.3, RD3, SK.5); current real-format time fixtures use local test-only TSA roots and locally mined headers. Retained revocation data, root pinning, independence groups, confirmation policy and archival renewal remain verifier responsibilities. Supported cryptographic algorithms are bounded by the [timestamp decision](../decisions/timestamp-adapter-decision.md); unsupported evidence is refused, never silently trusted.
- **Delivery/UI:** SK.1/SK.2 and WEB.1/WEB.2 implementation records do not close hosted-agent, complete browser-flow, accessibility or performance gates. [WEB.3](../../research/reviews/web3-bounded-gate-closure-99a1a8b.md) and [WEB.4](../../research/reviews/web4-bounded-gate-closure-c0c34cc.md) are bounded met on retained independent fixed-flow/testing-page evidence; [WEB.5 source-level closure](../../research/reviews/web5-source-gate-closure-proposal-4e092c8.md) is proposed pending independent retention/scope review; WEB.6 and I5 remain planned. These finite browser observations do not establish full Shelf static-app conformance. M5.3 covers the bounded synthetic native demo only; later release/claim reviews remain required. A static documentation website is not a hosted verification service.
- **Other media and methods:** audio/video/binary perceptual extraction, structural and keyed matching, resolver privacy and broader marking are scoping/research or owner-blocked work (AUD/VID proposals, BIN, STRUCT, KEYED, RES). Exact-byte registration of an artifact does not implement perceptual matching for its medium. The [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md) remains the complete gate ledger, including owner-blocked work and follow-ups; this release does not close any of those gates.
