## Both local operation boundaries

**Register:** authorized regular-file snapshots are hashed before consent and signed at those exact digests. Consent binds operation, bytes, full signing-key anchor, destination and optional claims; changing these requires fresh approval. `keyRef` is an explicitly selected local capability, not key bytes or a model-provided identity. The existing statement/nonce/possession/catalogue/DSSE rules are inherited. Complete bundle persistence precedes a success ID; ambiguous responses require inspecting a local receipt instead of blind signing retries. Post-commit cancellation must disclose the receipt rather than pretending no commit occurred. Each new completed registration has a fresh nonce and is not idempotent. No operation clock or queued timestamp submission manufactures proved time.

**Verify:** optional file/catalogue/key-event inputs are untrusted data; the independently selected local policy supplies trust. No signing key is requested. Missing optional artifacts preserve `not-supplied`, while unavailable required bundle input or invalid policy gives an operation error. Malformed/unsupported/invalid/untrusted/rejected evidence keeps distinct existing outcomes wherever evaluable. Verification changes neither bundle, key nor policy and does not acquire/renew evidence. Optional result persistence is disclosed as a separate consented local write. Retry semantics depend on identical inputs, policy snapshot and verification time, without a byte-identity claim.

**Authority, storage and output:** opaque IDs are scoped by store and caller, not bearer access tokens, global discovery keys or paths. Model input cannot grant new file/key/policy/service authority. Local full results and exact policy snapshots remain distinct from the sanitized model projection. Validity, trust, digest/possession, catalogue, evidence, time quorum and claimed/self-declared fields are not collapsed into “verified.” Diagnostics remain local rather than echoing secrets/paths. These are coherent proposed obligations, not demonstrated access controls, sandboxing, canary results or automatic extension of SK.1/SK.2. Stable snapshot mechanisms, resource quotas, transport signaling and atomic-store implementation remain later implementation work; their absence is not missing design evidence for this criterion.
