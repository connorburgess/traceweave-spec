## Boundaries

This is sufficient portable citation of the accepted bounded review chain, not publication of every private original protocol/deployment record. Omitted raw transcripts cannot be reconstructed from a hash or projection; this package does not claim otherwise. It retains no native executable/WASM binary, browser profile/cache, credential or provider account metadata. Original local artifacts remain privately retained by their owners.

Synthetic DOM DragEvent has `isTrusted:false`; physical drag/native chooser and AT acceptance are unproved. Local copied static assets differ from retained production assets. Source/loader/WASM association is not reproducible deployment or live-interaction proof. Finite native/WASM agreement is not an independent mathematical oracle, universal conformance, authenticated original source, producer/time proof, or full provenance-envelope construction. Compare's explicit report-body requirement is the closure scope; registration Verify is separate. No privacy/network-attempt/offline-reload, physical-device/visual/AT or WEB.6 budget/performance certification follows. WEB.4/5/6 and M3 holds remain separate. The new retention package itself awaits independent review.
