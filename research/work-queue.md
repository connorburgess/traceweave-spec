## Loop rule

1. Declare the gate IDs being worked on. A loop completes only when each closes by type: E met; R met or negative-result; O decision recorded, with committed evidence.
2. Checkpoints, evidence reviews and bounded progress records do not themselves close gates. A loop changing no gate status ends paused or blocked and names the gate.
3. Status and evidence change together, with enough committed evidence for a non-author recheck.
4. When owner-blocked, ask once and move to another authorized open gate. Do not invent a phase or bypass a hold.
