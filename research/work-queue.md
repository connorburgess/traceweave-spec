## Follow-on research decisions

1. Assess whether observed fragment failures justify a different alignment model before identity framing.
2. Design a small public-code eligibility census with explicit revision/license/provenance and no private source. Select its corpus and inspect data licensing before acquisition or redistribution.
3. Specify identity/context framing and its capacity costs only after eligible capacity and synchronization evidence exist. A keyed construction does not itself prevent transplant.
4. Develop grouped calibration and held-out full-query evaluation; do not claim low false-alert rates from dependent synthetic cases.
5. Propose normative changes only with compatibility and conformance evidence; maintain independent media-profile gates.

These are ordered decisions, not promises of feasibility. Negative results may reject a carrier rather than trigger increasingly elaborate encoding.

## Operating rules

Continue independent work when one item is blocked. Keep measured outcomes and unresolved questions separate. Preserve existing uncommitted work and local raw reports; no automatic publication, normative format change, private app inspection, paid services or new diagram. Report checkpoints without claiming the whole roadmap complete. Close supervision only at its declared phase boundary with a clear remaining-work report, or request user input when all authorized work depends on it.
